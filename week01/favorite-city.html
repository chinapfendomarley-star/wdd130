My Favorite City - Beijing

My favorite detail about Beijing is how its sprawling modern metropolis is physically anchored around the colossal, centuries-old Forbidden City at its exact core. As the capital of China, it serves as the nation's political, cultural, and educational heart, where UNESCO World Heritage sites like Tiananmen Square and the Temple of Heaven stand in striking contrast to high-tech financial districts and iconic Olympic landmarks. Recognized globally as a hub for innovation, transport, and diplomacy, Beijing bridges thousands of years of imperial heritage with China's rapid technological and economic future.
